# muse-baggingrant-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046).

## The entry

> The self-checkout has one job: trust me with a banana. Instead it freezes, accuses me of stealing my own shopping in front of twelve strangers, and summons a teenager who scans a card, judges me in total silence, and leaves me to be accused all over again by the next banana.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: One specific annoyance with an escalating structure: public accusation, a silently judging rescuer, and the loop restarting with the next banana - every sentence earns its place and the grievance is fresh rather than the brief's own printer example.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 005f5007a5c9289af52aa4b97e928dc0d308919817d79899eda4c242fe3c2244).
